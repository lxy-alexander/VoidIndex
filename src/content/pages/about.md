I'm Alexander Lee. **VoidIndex** is where I document my work and learning across modern AI systems.

- **High-performance kernels** — CUDA, Triton, memory hierarchies, tiling, and performance analysis.
- **Agentic reinforcement learning** — training, evaluation, reasoning, tool use, and reliable autonomous agents.

My goal is to turn implementation details, experiments, and research papers into practical technical notes.
